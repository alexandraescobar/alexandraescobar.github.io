Alexandra Escobar

UCR '29 Business Administration | Finance & Accounting 📚

Leasing Consultant at DWELL Apartment Homes

I am a first-generation Mexican-American student, creative, and aspiring business leader currently pursuing my Bachelor of Science in Business Administration with a concentration in Finance at the University of California, Riverside. My journey is driven by a desire to bring depth, perspective, and vibrant artistic expression into business environments, which are fields that have historically lacked diverse and female representation. For me, business is not merely about numbers, systems, or transactions; it is a canvas where strategy meets storytelling, innovation, and community impact.

Growing up as a first-generation student shaped my resilient mindset and passion for advocacy. In high school, I channeled this passion into grass-roots activism, hosting a campus sit-in protest that created a safe space for students and educators to share their lived experiences as immigrants and children of immigrants. The event brought awareness to pressing political movements and reinforced my belief that true leadership requires courage, vulnerability, and the amplification of underrepresented voices. That same spirit of advocacy continues to guide my academic and professional endeavors today.

Currently, I work as a Leasing Consultant at Dwell Apartment Homes in Riverside. In this role, I manage client relationships, lead property tours, navigate property management tools like Yardi Aspire and CRM IQ, and oversee crucial move-in and operational logistics. Serving as a primary point of contact for current and prospective residents has sharpened my problem-solving abilities, communication skills, and operational agility.

Alongside my professional experience, I have actively expanded my career horizons through competitive leadership programs. As an intern and active alumna of The Alliance Internship Program, where I was selected as 1 of only 35 students, I engaged in intensive corporate training alongside professionals at companies like Glenair, Night Media, and Deloitte. This experience culminated in presenting a Capstone project to an audience of over 100 industry leaders, refining my public speaking and executive presentation capabilities. My involvement in programs like USC Upward Bound further instilled in me a dedication to mentorship and peer empowerment.

Beyond logistics and finance, creativity is at the core of who I am. Whether designing marketing collateral, publishing a magazine, or exploring graphic design, I bring an artistic eye and fresh design sense to every project I undertake. On campus, I stay active in the Latino Business Student Association (LBSA) to build community and foster professional development, while also challenging myself physically through strength training in UCR Girl Gains.

Fluent in English and Spanish, alongside foundational studies in French and Korean, I bring a multicultural lens to everything I build. I am eager to continue blending analytical expertise, creative vision, and community leadership as I forge my path in the business world.
